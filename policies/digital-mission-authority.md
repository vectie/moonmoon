# Digital mission authority

MoonMoon owns deterministic digital mission simulation and evaluation.

It may:

- read a serialized, accepted MoonRobo digital-model integration receipt;
- read workspace-relative robot, terrain, illumination and calibration
  evidence;
- run and replay the existing MoonMoon mission model;
- write product-owned simulation, provenance and review evidence.

It may not:

- determine MoonRobo physical readiness;
- submit, approve or reconcile robot commands;
- claim global lunar coverage from a bounded route;
- turn a fixture, missing license record or unavailable external dataset into
  production evidence;
- silently reuse a partial or identity-conflicting session.

An accepted simulation remains a reviewable digital result.
`physical_readiness` is always false.
